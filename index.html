swipeClean — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

swipeClean helps you review and delete photos on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Your photos and files never leave your device. They are not uploaded, copied, or transmitted to us or to anyone else. swipeClean has no server of its own and we operate no backend that receives your data.

swipeClean is two separate apps that share a name: one for iPhone and iPad, and one for Mac. They do not behave the same way, so this policy describes each of them in turn. Anything said below about the iPhone and iPad app does not apply to the Mac app, and the other way around.

swipeClean for iPhone and iPad

Your photos

swipeClean asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your photos one at a time and delete the ones you choose. All of this happens on your device. Deletions go through iOS's own confirmation dialog, so nothing is ever removed without you approving it.

We never receive, store, or transmit your photos, videos, or any information contained in them.

Advertising

The iPhone and iPad app is free and displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve those ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, including advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads.

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We do not receive it and we cannot identify you from it. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's Privacy & Terms.

Tracking permission

swipeClean asks whether you want to allow tracking across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, swipeClean shows you a consent form, provided through Google's User Messaging Platform, before any personalised advertising is requested. You can review or change your choices at any time from Settings → Privacy Options inside the app.

Removing ads

The iPhone and iPad app offers a one-time in-app purchase that removes all advertising. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. Once purchased, no further ad requests are made from the app.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional and is only used to preserve your usage statistics if you delete and reinstall the app. If you choose to sign in, swipeClean stores the anonymous user identifier Apple provides, on your device and in your iCloud Keychain. We do not receive your name or email address, and this identifier is never sent to us.

You can delete this at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app, which erases the stored identifier and the statistics backed up with it.

Usage statistics

swipeClean keeps counts of how many photos you have kept, deleted, and swiped, along with the storage space freed. These are stored on your device. If you are signed in with Apple, they are also backed up to your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage and your iCloud Keychain, so that they survive a reinstall and follow you to a new device. They are shown only to you, we cannot read them, and we collect no analytics of any kind.

swipeClean for Mac

A separate app, sold separately. It contains no advertising, no accounts and no in-app purchases of any kind.

The Mac app works in one of two modes, which you choose when you open it: your Apple Photos library, or a folder you pick yourself.

Photos library mode

swipeClean asks for access to your photo library so it can show you your photos one at a time. Photos you mark are collected and deleted together at the end, through macOS's own confirmation dialog, so nothing is removed without you approving it. All of this happens on your Mac, and we never receive, store, or transmit your photos or videos.

Local folder mode

In this mode swipeClean works on ordinary files — for example the contents of a memory card, a downloads folder, or an external drive.

  • swipeClean can only see folders you choose yourself, through the standard macOS folder picker. The app is sandboxed by macOS and has no access to any other part of your disk.
  • It remembers the folders you chose, so you do not have to pick them again next time. What is saved on your Mac is a reference to the folder, not a copy of anything inside it.
  • Inside a folder you chose, swipeClean reads image and video files in order to display them, and reads their dates in order to put them in sequence.
  • When you press an arrow key, swipeClean does exactly one thing to exactly one file: leaves it alone, moves it into another folder you chose, or puts it in the Finder Trash. Trashed files stay in the Trash until you empty it yourself.
  • Unlike the photo library, there is no separate confirmation step here — each keystroke acts straight away. Nothing is permanent, though: every action can be undone with the Z key, which moves the file back where it came from.
  • The contents of your files are never uploaded, copied off your Mac, or sent to us or anyone else.

No advertising, accounts, or statistics

The Mac app shows no advertisements. It does not ask for tracking permission, because it does no tracking. It has no sign-in, keeps no usage statistics, collects no analytics, and offers no in-app purchases.

No network access

The Mac app is sandboxed and is not granted permission to use the network at all. It cannot open a network connection, so nothing it reads from your photos or your folders can be sent anywhere. This is enforced by macOS itself, not merely a promise from us.

Both apps

Children

swipeClean is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
